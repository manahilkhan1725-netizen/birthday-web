# Birthday Cinema 🎬

An exact, luxury cinematic interactive birthday website replica based on the video demonstration.

## ✨ Features & Chapters Included

1. **Screen 1: The Intro / Curtain Raiser**
   - Subtitle: `PRESENTING A SPECIAL STORY`
   - Headline: *"Tonight, one story deserves to be celebrated..."* with gold serif italic typography.
   - Tagline: `A STORY ABOUT YOU ❤️`
   - Interactive `▶ PLAY THE MOVIE` button.

2. **Screen 2: Vintage Cinema Ticket (Special Pass)**
   - Authentic gold/parchment ticket card with cutout perforated notches and dashed tear line.
   - Details: `SPECIAL PASS`, `BIRTHDAY 2026`, `ADMIT ONE [NAME]`, `ONE NIGHT ONLY`, and `BD-26` with cake icon.
   - `TEAR THE TICKET ✂️` button with physics-based splitting & tear sound effects.

3. **Screen 3: Chapter 01 — "The Story Begins..."**
   - Elegant typography with the poetic quote: *"Every beautiful story begins with a single moment."*
   - Smooth navigation arrow button to continue.

4. **Screen 4: Chapter 02 — "The Memory Film" (35mm Celluloid Reel Strip)**
   - Classic retro film strip borders with square sprocket holes and frame numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`).
   - Horizontal carousel with active zoom, touch swipe, navigation arrows, and `01 / 04` counter.

5. **Screen 5: Chapter 03 — "Things I Wish For You"**
   - 2-column frosted glass cards:
     1. **Happiness** — More reasons to smile every single day.
     2. **Dreams** — May your biggest dreams find their way to you.
     3. **Adventure** — New places, new experiences and beautiful stories.
     4. **Peace** — A heart that feels calm, loved and content.
     5. **Beautiful Memories** — Moments you'll remember for years to come.
     6. **Genuine Smiles** — Because your happiness truly matters.

6. **Screen 6: Chapter 04 — Private Letter & 3D Envelope**
   - Crimson velvet envelope with a golden heart wax seal.
   - Tap to open: Flap flips open and the parchment letter slides out smoothly.
   - Custom personalized letter text with signature.

7. **Screen 7: The Birthday Trailer (Kinetic Typography)**
   - Cinematic fast-paced keyword cuts: `SMILE.`, `BELIEVE.`, `CREATE.`, `DREAM.`, `LOVE.` with motion blur, sound hits, and smooth transition.

8. **Screen 8: The Big Reveal & Interactive Birthday Cake**
   - Glowing multi-tier birthday cake with 3 animated flickering candle flames.
   - `MAKE A WISH 🕯️` button / tap candles: Extinguishes flames with smoke effect, releases celebration audio fanfare and a massive confetti burst!

9. **Screen 9: Hollywood Rolling End Credits**
   - Authentic movie end credits with smooth roll:
     - `STARRING: [NAME]`
     - `WRITTEN BY: Beautiful Memories`
     - `PRODUCED BY: Love & Happiness`
     - `MUSIC & VIBES: Laughter & Celebration`
     - `DIRECTED BY: You ❤️`
     - `THE END... OR MAYBE A NEW BEGINNING ✨`
     - `WATCH AGAIN ↺` button to restart the show anytime.

10. **Bonus Features**:
    - **Original Music**: Extracted soundtrack (`bgm.mp3`) with floating music toggle button and animated sound wave.
    - **Sound Synthesizer**: Web Audio API sound effects for ticket tearing, film reel clicking, envelope rustle, and celebration fanfares (works completely offline).
    - **Personalization Editor**: Click the **Edit** button in the top left to instantly customize the recipient's name, celebration year, letter text, or upload custom photos.

## 🚀 How to Run

Simply open [index.html](file:///c:/Users/user/OneDrive/Desktop/app/index.html) in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox), or run a local server:

```powershell

node server.js
```
Then visit `http://localhost:3000`.
